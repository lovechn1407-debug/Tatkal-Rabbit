tips_and_updates Tatkal Rabbit – Guide, Tips & FAQ
play_circle How It Works (Automated Workflow)
  1. Pre-fill Details: Save your Stations, Travel Class, Quota, and Passenger details before Tatkal opening time.
  2. Tatkal Clock Sync: Click "Start IRCTC Automation" or use the scheduled timer 30 seconds before opening (10 AM for AC, 11 AM for Non-AC).
  3. Auto-Search & Instant Avail: Tatkal Rabbit launches IRCTC, searches trains, checks availability the exact second the window opens, and clicks "Book Now".
  4. Autofill Form & Fast Pay: Passengers and preferences are filled in under 1 second. You enter the CAPTCHA, and your selected UPI gateway opens for instant payment.
bolt Tatkal Pro Tips (Speed Secrets)
  • Login 2–3 Mins Early: Login around 09:57 AM (for AC) or 10:57 AM (for Non-AC) to prevent session timeouts.
  • Use Paytm / PhonePe QR: UPI QR Code checkout is the fastest checkout method on IRCTC, completely avoiding bank OTP delays.
  • Leave Train Number Empty: If you want whatever train is available fastest on the route, leave the train number blank.
  • Keep Passenger Names Under 16 Chars: IRCTC trims long names; pre-fill short names as per govt ID to avoid errors.
warning_amber Important Cautions & IRCTC Rules
  • Tatkal Max Limit: IRCTC strictly limits Tatkal bookings to maximum 4 passengers per PNR.
  • Manual CAPTCHA Requirement: Per IRCTC security regulations, CAPTCHAs require your manual glance & entry. Tatkal Rabbit chimes and pauses for you to type it instantly.
  • Do Not Refresh During Payment: Once on the UPI / QR payment screen, do not reload the browser tab.
quiz Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
  • Q: What are the subscription plans?
    A: Tatkal Rabbit offers 3 high-speed passes: 2-Day Express (₹49), Weekly Explorer (₹119), and Monthly Pro (₹299). Active passes instantly unlock all autofill and automation options on your Chrome extension.
  • Q: Is my IRCTC Password safe?
    A: Yes. Credentials are encrypted locally using AES-GCM and stored only inside your Chrome browser's local sandbox. No sensitive credentials ever leave your device.
  • Q: What is the difference between Old Site and New Site?
    A: Old Site (/nget) is lightweight and often withstands 10:00 AM traffic spikes better. New Site (/eticket) features IRCTC's newer interface. Both work seamlessly.
privacy_tip Privacy & Security Policy

Tatkal Rabbit operates 100% locally on your computer. It does not track, collect, store, or transmit your personal details, journey information, payment data, or IRCTC credentials to any remote server or third party. Everything resides securely in your browser's private extension storage.
