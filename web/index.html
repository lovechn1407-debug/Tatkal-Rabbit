Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do subscriptions work with Tatkal Rabbit?

When you sign in with Google in the extension or web client, you can choose a 2-Day (₹49), Weekly (₹119), or Monthly (₹299) pass. Once activated, the Chrome extension unlocks all automation features instantly.

Is my IRCTC account password secure?

Absolutely. Credentials never leave your computer. Tatkal Rabbit stores passwords locally using client-side AES-GCM encryption within Chrome's isolated storage sandbox.

Why does Tatkal Rabbit use UPI QR checkout?

Credit card and Netbanking gateways on IRCTC frequently suffer from OTP SMS delays during peak 10:00 AM rush. UPI QR code opens instantly on screen, allowing you to scan and approve in seconds.

How do I switch between the Old Site and New Site?

In the extension header bar, click "Old Site" (/nget) or "New Site" (/eticket). The extension will automatically adjust all selectors and navigations accordingly.
